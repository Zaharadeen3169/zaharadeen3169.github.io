Privacy Policy

Pan Tech Africa · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Pan Tech Africa ("we", "us") is an independent online marketplace for technology products. We are not owned by, endorsed by or affiliated with Pi Network. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Pi account information: your Pi username and a unique Pi user ID, provided through Pi sign-in with your permission.
  • Order and delivery details: your name, phone number, delivery address, city, state and delivery instructions, collected only to fulfil your order.
  • Payment records: the Pi payment reference, transaction ID, amount and status.
  • Support messages and reviews you send us.

2. What we never collect

We never ask for, collect, store or see your Pi wallet passphrase or private keys. Do not share your passphrase with anyone, including us.

3. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders and confirm Pi payments.
  • To provide order tracking, notifications and customer support.
  • To handle returns and refunds.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the marketplace secure.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share delivery details only with the people who deliver your order, and we may share information where required by law. Payments are processed through the Pi payment system.

5. Storage and security

Your data is stored on servers accessible only to authorised staff. We use access controls and server-side payment verification. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed for fulfilment, accounting and legal obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping duties. We aim to handle requests in line with applicable data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where it applies.

8. Children

Our service is not intended for children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
